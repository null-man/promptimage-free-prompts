## How to use a prompt

1. Copy a prompt from a collection.
2. Replace the bracketed fields with your subject, brand colors, or setting.
3. Generate a first version in [Prompt Image](https://promptimage.cloud/image).
4. Change one visual choice at a time—usually lighting, composition, or material—to refine the result.

The strongest results usually come from a clear subject, a specific setting, a visual style, lighting, composition, and one priority such as clean background, readable packaging, or natural texture.
