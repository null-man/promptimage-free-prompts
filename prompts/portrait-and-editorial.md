# Portrait and Editorial Prompts

## 4. Quiet editorial portrait

```text
editorial portrait of [person], [wardrobe], standing beside a large window, soft overcast daylight, 85mm lens perspective, gentle film grain, understated [color] palette, relaxed expression, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture
```

## 5. Fashion campaign frame

```text
full-length fashion photograph of [person] wearing [outfit], walking through [setting], low afternoon sun, long clean shadows, cinematic composition, subtle motion in fabric, modern magazine styling, realistic detail, vertical 4:5 framing
```

## 6. Creative studio profile

```text
creative professional at a worktable with [tools or materials], calm studio environment, soft directional light, tactile surfaces, documentary photography style, honest expression, balanced composition, muted warm tones, high-resolution detail
```
